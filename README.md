## The word

**Nanomanager** — the boss who doesn't merely micromanage, but inspects
every atom of your work, at nanometre scale. Funny on its own (an
escalation of the familiar "micromanager"), true of any boss who nitpicks
the smallest details, and fresh: no other entry in the field uses it.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, deterministic, no network)
writes exactly that one word — 11 characters, no trailing newline — to
`/output/entry.txt`, after validating it against the competition gate
(1–30 characters, a single word, not only whitespace).

```sh
python3 main.py
```
